Goal: Create a cinematic, interactive 3D Indian wedding invitation that feels like a Bollywood title sequence rather than a traditional website.

Project Vision
Create a cinematic experience that tells the story of Nikita and Ankit through an interactive 3D journey inspired by Rajasthan, Lord Krishna, royal palaces and handcrafted storybooks.

Core Principles
• Narrative over navigation
• One seamless cinematic journey
• Premium visual quality
• Mobile-first performance
• Reusable architecture
• Feature-based implementation
• Accessibility and graceful degradation

To keep quality consistently high, I'd build it in phases rather than trying to do everything at once.
